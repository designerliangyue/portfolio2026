# Portfolio claim-to-evidence register

This internal checklist keeps public claims precise. It is not intended for publication.

| Public claim | Evidence currently represented in the portfolio | Boundary / review rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10+ years | Career timeline runs from 2016 to 2026 | Update the number only when the timeline supports it |
| Shipped work across 7 countries | Owner-confirmed professional history | Keep the country list private unless individual projects can be disclosed |
| AI Sales Co-Pilot launched | Three production flows: performance inquiry, marketing assets, conversational quoting | Do not imply adoption, conversion, or revenue impact without records |
| AI research direction | Meta documentation, benchmark services, live WABA environment, existing workflows, partner input | Indirect research; no direct Indonesian-agent interviews claimed |
| Ignite research | 10 Indonesian and 15 Vietnamese agents with local researchers; seven-product competitive audit | Keep sample and market scope attached to the claim |
| Ignite feedback | 60-day window, 300+ responses, 73.5% satisfaction, NPS score 67 | Product feedback signals, not design-only or revenue impact |
| Ignite shipped modules | Grow, Recruitment, Contest, Survey | Use delivery language; do not infer adoption |
| CMB private-cloud delivery | IA, operator workflows, table patterns, onboarding, light/dark component system | No verified efficiency or bank-wide adoption claim |
| WCAG | Contrast checked against WCAG criteria | Do not call the full product WCAG compliant without a complete audit |
| Employee Benefits | Stakeholder model, feature map, flows, wireframes, interactive prototypes | Interview sample, usability-test record, launch state, and business impact are not documented |

Before publishing future edits, every new number, adoption statement, or causal outcome should be added here with its source and disclosure boundary.
